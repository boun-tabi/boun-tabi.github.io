# Utilizing Digital Technology for Social Cohesion

This project focuses on positive messaging and peace by boosting collaboration, exchange, and solidarity. It is led by the Hrant Dink Foundation and involves Sabanci University and Bogazici University. The project includes research on NLP hate speech detection in social media.

**Keywords:** NLP, Hate Speech Detection, Social Media

**Funding:** European Union - EuropeAid (2021-2024)
