We are delighted to announce that **PUFFIN: protein unit discovery with functional supervision** has been accepted for publication in *Bioinformatics*.

PUFFIN is a data-driven framework that discovers structurally coherent, function-aware units in proteins by combining graph-based structural partitioning with functional supervision.

Congratulations to Gökçe Uludoğan, Buse Giledereli, Elif Ozkirimli, and Arzucan Özgür!
