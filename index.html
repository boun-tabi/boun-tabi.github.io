Welcome to TABILab

TABILab is a research group developing machine learning methods for both language and biological data. Our projects span natural language processing and bioinformatics.

Natural Language Processing (NLP)

We study linguistic structure, information extraction, and language modeling, with a particular focus on Turkish and other low-resource languages.

Bioinformatics

We build computational representations of proteins, genes, and compounds to study interactions, annotate function, and support molecular design, exploring new ways to understand the “language of life” embedded in biological sequences.

Our work is grounded in interdisciplinary collaboration and high-quality datasets that enable robust, theory-informed models across both domains.
